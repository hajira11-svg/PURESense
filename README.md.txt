# PURESense
### Portable Unified Rapid Evaluation Sensor for Food Authenticity Screening

> **Sense the sample. Learn the fingerprint. Flag the anomaly.**

PURESense is a proposed low-cost, portable multisensor platform for **rapid preliminary food-authenticity screening**.

The system combines **optical, electrical, and thermal sensing** with signal processing and machine learning to characterize a food sample through a multidimensional **physical sensor fingerprint**.

Instead of relying on a single sensor threshold, PURESense combines complementary measurements and evaluates whether a new sample resembles the learned reference population.

**PURESense is designed to screen, not certify.** A suspicious or uncertain result should be followed by laboratory verification.

---

## 1. Problem

Food can look normal even when its physical or chemical characteristics differ from an expected reference.

Consumers and field inspectors may not have access to rapid, low-cost testing at the point where a suspicious sample is encountered.

The conventional pathway is:

Suspected Sample
       ↓
Laboratory Testing
       ↓
Waiting / Analysis
       ↓
Verified Result
